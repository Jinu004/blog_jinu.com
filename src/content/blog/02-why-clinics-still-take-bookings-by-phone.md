If you run a small clinic, most of your appointments probably come in over the phone. There are good reasons for that, and they are worth stating honestly before talking about anything else.

## Why the phone works

**It is fast.** A patient calls, someone answers, a time is agreed and the call is over. No forms, no waiting for a reply.

**Patients trust it.** Hearing a real person say "come at five" feels certain. The patient knows the booking is made because they heard it being made.

**There is nothing to set up.** The clinic already has a phone and someone at the desk. No software, no training, no new habit to build.

None of this is a mistake. The phone is a sensible default.

## What it costs

The costs of phone booking are real, but they are quiet, which makes them easy to miss.

**Missed calls.** When the desk is busy with a patient standing in front of them, the phone rings out. Some callers try again. Some do not, and you never find out who they were.

**After hours.** Some patients think about booking in the evening or early in the morning, when the clinic is closed. A call at that time goes nowhere, and a message may sit unanswered until the next day.

**A busy desk.** The person who answers the phone is often the same person greeting patients, handling files and taking payments. Every booking call pulls them away from the person at the counter.

## A one-week exercise

Before deciding whether any of this matters for your clinic, measure it. It takes one week and a notebook.

1. **Count missed calls.** At the end of each day, check the phone's call log and write down how many calls were not answered.
2. **Count after-hours messages.** Each morning, write down how many calls and WhatsApp messages came in while the clinic was closed.
3. **Keep going for seven days**, writing both numbers down every day.

At the end of the week, add them up. If the totals are small, the phone is working well for you and there is no need to change anything. If they are bigger than you expected, you now know what phone booking is costing you, in your own clinic, with your own numbers.

Either way, you will be deciding from what you counted rather than from a guess.
